• In quantum mechanics, the measurement of momentum and position is incompatible.

the same time.
• Note that measurement of the momentum means to collapse the state into a momentum
basis vector, which happens to be the momentum in our daily experience, and
measurement of position means to collapse the particle state into a position basis vector,ฅ

• There is a valid reason why we cannot measure the momentum and position with
